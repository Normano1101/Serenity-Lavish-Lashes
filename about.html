About Me

Ever since I was young, I’ve been fascinated by the beauty of eyelashes and how they enhance a person’s look. In 2023, I became a licensed lash technician, turning my passion into a career dedicated to making clients feel confident and beautiful. I specialize in classic, wet, volume, hybrid, and wispy lash sets, customizing each look to complement my clients' unique features. With a strong attention to detail, I ensure every set is flawless while providing a comfortable and relaxing experience. I love meeting new clients, personalizing each visit to match their aesthetic, and creating a welcoming space for everyone, regardless of background or gender. Outside of lashing, I’m a big animal lover—especially when it comes to dogs! I look forward to enhancing your lashes and giving you a confidence boost with every blink.
